Women were taught that pleasure is something to hide. That silence — not the product — is the real barrier. Nancy exists to end it, and this strategy is how.

This is a belief problem before it's a reach problem. Every pillar in this plan is built to take one of these down.

Answer: "Nancy Explains" — plain, save-worthy anatomy & technique.

Toys still read as dirty, a threat, or something only "other" women do.
Answer: real voices & stories that widen who feels invited.

The fear isn't the toy — it's being caught wanting one.

## 🗣️ Unspoken desire
She wants to ask a partner — no one taught her how.
Answer: exact scripts + "a plus-one, not a replacement."

## The through-line
Nancy already has the attention. This strategy turns it into belief — because here, you don't win by shouting louder. You win by being the brand that makes pleasure safe to talk about.
