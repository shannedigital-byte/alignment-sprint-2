Your own data names the customer. Public creative — Miami Swim Week, pickleball, Pride, Lottie Moss — reads to 20–35. That audience is worth having; it is not the one paying.

+
Working
  • Demographic truth is quantified, not guessed — a 146,000-response dataset leadership quotes publicly.
  • "My Grandma Owns This" (69-year-old ambassador, Miami) is the one on-strategy activation, and it used humour rather than sympathy. Correct instinct.
  • Product already serves 45+ better than marketing does: single-button operation, <50dB, clinical framing on menopause and post-cancer responsiveness.
−
Not working
  • Casting skews ~20 years younger than the buyer. A 52-year-old is asked to buy from a brand visibly not addressing her.
  • Zero content for the male gift-buyer — 20% of revenue, no journey, no answer to the objection that blocks it.
  • Channel weight inverts demographics: 67% of owned audience on IG; FB and YouTube (where 45+ research) graded D and F.
  • Grandma Lori ran once. Best-fitting asset of the year, treated as a moment not a franchise.
→
Recommendations
  • 40% casting floor — minimum share of organic creative featuring women 45+, reviewed monthly.
  • Launch the "Second Act" pillar — menopause, post-cancer sensation change, long-marriage intimacy, dating after 50.
  • Build the male gift-buyer track — short series on how to give it and what it doesn't mean. FB + YouTube, weighted Q4 and Valentine's.
  • Retain Grandma Lori on a rolling agreement with a monthly recurring format.

+
Working
  • "No sexual imagery" is a growth lever, not a compromise — it buys ad approval, retail viability, giftability and shareability at once.
  • Six-character system is an unrun franchise. Each SKU can carry a voice, a rivalry, a running joke.
  • Identity is tightly specified and consistently applied — Fraunces display, ES Rebond Grotesque text, pink #FF30CC, cream #FCF7ED, lime #BCFF00, soft 3rem geometry.
  • Copy discipline is high and legally safe — "Break taboo, embrace pleasure," "She Comes First."
−
Not working
  • Every highlight is a tentpole. No education series, no community feature, nothing a follower can anticipate weekly.
  • Characters are introduced then abandoned — "meet LEM/AVO/BERRi/Uno" are launch cards, not ongoing equity.
  • Voice is tuned to the converted. "If you're here, you better come" is a closed door to a nervous 50-year-old first-timer. No beginner on-ramp.
  • LinkedIn shares no voice DNA with the flagship — same brand, two companies.
→
Recommendations
  • Fix a weekly pillar ratio: Education 40% / Community & UGC 25% / Character 20% / Product 15%. Campaigns sit on top, never instead.
  • Write a one-page character bible per fruit; first recurring format = fruits answering real customer questions.
  • Add a second, gentler voice register for beginner and 45+ content. Current voice becomes the arrival point, not the entry requirement.
  • Port the brand voice to LinkedIn — corporate register is suppressing an inherently interesting story.

+
Working
  • Customers overcome real social friction to praise you publicly — 4.7 across 15,000+ reviews in an embarrassment-driven category is unusually strong signal.
  • UGC is earned, not bought. Lem's TikTok virality was organic — the product itself generates content.
  • On-site testimonials are specific and credible, not generic.
−
Not working
  • No UGC pipeline. No repost cadence, no branded hashtag, no creator-to-customer loop. Every organic video is a one-off.
  • The 28-point rating gap is a live conversion risk — 66% on Facebook vs 94% equivalent on Trustpilot, sitting on your core demographic's channel.
  • Reddit unowned — the highest-trust surface in this category, where the real purchase decision is litigated.
  • Relationship ends at delivery. No group, newsletter community or membership for customers who want to keep talking.
  • Unverified adjacent storefront — hellonancys.shop / care@hellonancys.com, unlinked from the main site. Could not be confirmed brand-owned from public evidence. Flagged for internal check, Section 09.
→
Recommendations
  • One branded hashtag + a fixed weekly repost slot, promoted in-box and in post-purchase email.
  • Read and resolve all 35 Facebook reviews, then solicit reviews from satisfied customers. Fast, cheap, measurable.
  • Governed Reddit presence — verified, clearly labelled, answers questions, never sells.
  • Open a moderated community group — private Facebook group is the right first test given the 45+ skew.
  • Add a physical unboxing prompt card to the pink box. Cheapest UGC lever available; fits the existing ritual.

Nick Kyrgios as the first male athlete to publicly back a female pleasure brand is a genuinely original strategic idea. The roster — Lottie Moss, a Regent's Park pop-up, an MP-attended "Women Come First" dinner, GPs, an FACOG and a menopause specialist — is not quickly replicable.

+
Working
  • Kyrgios has a real thesis behind it — men as participants, not beneficiaries. Same-day press, and it directly serves the 20% male gift-buyer.
  • Expert credibility de-risks the cautious 45+ buyer — category-leading and hard to buy quickly.
  • Policy-adjacent work differentiates — convening MPs on lifelong sex education positions you as advocate, not vendor.
−
Not working
  • 13 clinicians deployed as static PDP quotes. Zero series, Q&As or takeovers.
  • Activations aren't merchandised. 1,000 attendees and national coverage; little evidence of the follow-on content programme.
  • Founder equity unmanaged — founder post 33 reactions vs brand post 4; third-party post 63. The people out-reach the page 8–15×.
  • No always-on creator tier beneath the celebrity tier — the most expensive way to buy reach.
→
Recommendations
  • Batch-film a monthly expert format ("Ask a Sexologist"), cut for Reels, Shorts and Facebook. Highest-leverage unused asset in the account.
  • Set a 1:6 activation-to-content ratio — every tentpole ships six weeks of derivative content, planned before the event.
  • Formalise founder-led LinkedIn; brand page amplifies rather than competes.
  • Recruit 20–30 micro-creators (45+ and clinician-adjacent) on rolling gifting to hold reach between tentpoles.

Working

  • 100+ education articles on a domain you control — platform-proof reach already paid for.
  • Third-party legitimacy — 2025 Women's Wellness Tech Award; coverage in Vice, The Guardian, SheKnows, The Sun.
−
Not working
  • Unmanaged concentration risk. Growth depends on Meta and TikTok; a single policy revision could remove most reach in a week. No contingency visible.
  • 3.7% owned buffer is too thin to absorb a restriction event.
  • 100+ articles not feeding social — a finished content supply chain sitting idle.
  • Four externally visible hygiene defects: duplicate LinkedIn post · unlinked YouTube channel · a second "Hello Nancy" entity in LinkedIn's own suggestions · unverified adjacent domain.
→
Recommendations
  • Write the platform contingency plan now, while nothing is wrong. Name the owned channels that absorb a restriction.
  • Make owned-audience capture per order a board metric, tracked weekly.
  • Repurpose every article into a carousel, a Short and a Pinterest pin. Strategy already done and paid for.
  • Consolidate properties: verify the adjacent domain, resolve the duplicate LinkedIn entity, link YouTube from the footer, adopt a publishing checklist.

09
Data
request

To convert inference into verified finding

→
Required from client
  • Native analytics, 12 months — IG, TikTok, FB, YouTube: reach, engagement rate, saves, shares, follower age/gender by channel.
  • Paid media — spend by channel and market, ROAS, CAC, creative-level performance, history of ad rejections or restrictions.
  • Commerce attribution — Shopify sessions and revenue by social source, to rank channels on contribution rather than engagement.
  • The 146,000-response survey dataset — so casting and pillars are set against your evidence, not category assumption.
  • Property confirmation — is hellonancys.shop brand-owned? Which LinkedIn entity is canonical? Who holds YouTube credentials?
  • Resourcing — social headcount, production capacity, approval process, so the roadmap is scoped to what you can ship.
Recommendation

Don't wait for the data to start. All four Days 1–30 actions hold under any plausible reading of the numbers, and three cost effectively nothing to begin.
